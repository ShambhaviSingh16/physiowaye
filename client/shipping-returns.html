Shipping and returns

Delivery timing, serviceability and return options can depend on the product and destination. Please contact us before paying if you need these details confirmed for your order.

Delivery

The checkout page shows the amount payable before payment. Once a shipment is created, its courier updates appear in Track Your Order. Contact us with your PW order reference if you need a dispatch estimate or help with an address.

Problems with an order

If an item arrives damaged or differs from your order, contact physiowaye@gmail.com with your PW order reference and photos of the item and packaging. Our team will review the issue and explain the next steps. Please ask before sending equipment back so it reaches the correct address.

Cancellations, other returns and warranty

Contact the team before purchase or as soon as possible after ordering to confirm the options for the specific equipment, including any return window, refund timing and warranty coverage.
