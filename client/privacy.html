Store information

Privacy information

When you use the store, Physiowaye receives the information needed to operate your account, answer enquiries and fulfil orders.

Information you provide

This can include your name, email, phone number, delivery address, order details and messages sent through the contact form. A guest shopping cart is stored in your browser until you sign in or clear it.

How it is used

We use this information for account access, checkout, payment confirmation, delivery updates and customer support. The website uses Supabase for account and store data, Razorpay for payments and Delhivery for shipment tracking. If you choose Google sign-in, Google participates in that sign-in process.

Card payment details are entered in the Razorpay payment window. The store keeps the payment status and limited payment reference or masked method details needed to identify an order.

Your questions or requests

For a copy or correction of your details, or to ask about deletion and retention, email physiowaye@gmail.com. Some order and payment records may need to be retained to handle completed purchases.
